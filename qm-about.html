ABOUT US

Our background

QM Studio is a digital content and services company, offering books and book-related services in Nilai, founded by graduates of the Quran with Multimedia programme at USIM.

We provide digital-oriented services, specialising in podcast services for academics such as university lecturers to share their research with the public.

The company's aspiration is to become a platform dedicated to serving lecturers, in step with technological developments and in line with the mission of universities in Malaysia to spread Islamic knowledge and modern skills more widely.
