Intro

QM Studio specialises in podcast services for academics, from topic and question planning and recording to editing and digital distribution.

Lecturers spend years on grants, research and books, yet the results often stay locked in journals that few people read. You receive podcast episodes and short video clips that bring your research to students, peers and the wider public.

01How do I make a booking?

You can visit the kiosk (walk-in) or book online through WhatsApp 011-10724719 or email qmstudioofficialmy@gmail.com.

02Who is this service suitable for?

This service is tailored for USIM staff, lecturers and students, especially academics who want to share their research.

03Where are the videos published?

Episodes are uploaded to QM Studio YouTube, while short clips are shared on QM Studio TikTok.

04What is 1MSE?

1MSE is short content that highlights a lecturer's expertise. It is included free in Package 1 and Package 2, together with the Lecturer Profile and Book or Article Promo.

05Can a video be taken down after it is published?

Yes. The admin can take down a video at the request of the panel or a statutory body, effective within 1–7 days of broadcast (optional).

06Can I keep the video clips?

In Package 3, the panel may keep all .mp4 video clips for personal sharing (WhatsApp) without altering the original content, and is responsible for sharing on their own platforms.

07How much do the packages cost?

Introductory promo prices: Package 1 RM 500, Package 2 RM 1,500 and Package 3 RM 5,000.
